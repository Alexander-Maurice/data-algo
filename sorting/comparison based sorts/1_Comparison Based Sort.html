Comparison Based Sort

Comparison based sorts are sorting algorithms that require a direct method of comparison defined by the ordering relation. In a sense, they are the most natural sorting algorithms since, intuitively, when we think about sorting elements, we instinctively think about comparing elements to each other. In the following sections, we’ll introduce some of the fundamental comparison based sorting algorithms.
